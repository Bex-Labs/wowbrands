Operational playbook

How we actually work together.

Four unshakeable operating truths that prevent bloat, eliminate vendor bureaucracy, and keep our standard uncompromising.

01 Standard of delivery

The reaction is the brief.

If a plan doesn't produce a visible reaction — a laugh, a gasp, a headline — we haven't finished the brief yet. Decks are cheap; tangible audience pulse is the only currency we bill for.

Zero lukewarm executions permitted

02 Structural integration

One team, not three vendors.

Agency, Surprises, and Impact sit in the same room on every project, so nothing gets lost between departments. No handoffs to outsourced junior white-labels.

Single chain of creative custody

03 Radical empathy

Small details, said out loud.

We ask what a client's team actually calls things, then use those names — not our own internal jargon. The deepest surprises exist in the micro-nuances only insiders recognize.

Vernacular matching & forensic listening

04 Enduring reputation

Play the long game.

We'd rather lose a quarter's revenue than a client's trust. Retainers here are measured in years, not months. We advise against bad activations even when they are lucrative.

Integrity over short-term billables
